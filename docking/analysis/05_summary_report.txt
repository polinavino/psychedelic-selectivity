Docking Summary Report
=================================================================
n = 31 compounds (22 tryptamines, 9 phenethylamines)

Overall:        Pearson r = +0.428  p = 0.0163
                Spearman rho = +0.471  p = 0.0075

Tryptamines:    r = -0.243  p = 0.2767  (n=22)
Phenethylamines:r = +0.800  p = 0.0096  (n=9)

Scaffold score gap: tryptamine mean = -6.665, phenethylamine mean = -5.694
  t-test: t = -6.07, p = 1.33e-06

Interpretation:
  Overall r = +0.428 is positive — WRONG direction.
  A predictive model should have r < 0 (better docking = higher pKi).
  The positive correlation is driven by scaffold separation:
    tryptamines dock better (mean -6.67)
    phenethylamines dock worse (mean -5.69)
  but phenethylamines have comparable or higher measured pKi.
  DOB (pKi=8.27) and DOI (pKi=8.28) are among the strongest binders
  yet dock at only -5.57 and -5.66 kcal/mol.

  Within phenethylamines: r = +0.800 — strong positive correlation,
  again wrong direction. The best binders (2C-B, DOB, DOI) dock worst.

  Cause: 6WHA was co-crystallised with 25CN-NBOH, a tryptamine-like
  compound. The orthosteric pocket geometry is optimised for tryptamine
  scaffolds. Phenethylamines bind through a different mode not captured
  by this structure.

  This is the 3D analogue of the QSAR scaffold confounding finding:
  structure-based virtual screening at 6WHA will systematically favour
  tryptamines over phenethylamines regardless of true affinity.
