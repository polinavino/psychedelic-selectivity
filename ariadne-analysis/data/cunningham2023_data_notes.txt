Column definitions
------------------
compound      : compound name as used in the paper
assay         : gq_bret     = Gq protein dissociation BRET2 assay (HEK293T cells)
                barr2_bret  = beta-arrestin2 recruitment BRET2 assay
                ca_flux     = Ca2+ flux assay (Flp-In 293 T-Rex cells)
                ki          = radioligand displacement ([125I]-DOI)
receptor      : serotonin receptor subtype
ec50_nM       : EC50 in nM (or Ki in nM for ki assay); from nonlinear regression
emax_pct      : Emax as % of 5-HT response at same receptor/assay; blank for ki rows
source_figure : figure in the paper where this value appears
notes         : compound class, stereochemistry notes, etc.

Assay details
-------------
All BRET assays: BRET2 in HEK293T cells, transfected 48h before assay.
  Gq dissociation: 1:1:1:1 ratio receptor:Ga-Rluc8:Beta:GFP2-gamma
  beta-arrestin2:  1:7.5 ratio 5-HT-Rluc8:GFP2-fused-beta-Arrestin2
  Coelenterazine 400a substrate, 60 min drug incubation at 37C
  Data normalised to % 5-HT positive control
Ca2+ flux: Flp-In 293 T-Rex stable cell lines, Fluo-4 Direct dye, FLIPR Tetra
Ki: radioligand displacement with [125I]-DOI at human 5-HT2A

Key results from paper
-----------------------
- Ariadne is non-hallucinogenic in humans up to 300 mg (clinical data)
- DOM is hallucinogenic at 3-5 mg in humans
- Mechanism: NOT biased agonism. Ariadne shows equal drop in potency and
  efficacy at both Gq and beta-arrestin2 (~4-6 fold weaker potency vs DOM,
  ~15-20% lower Emax across all pathways)
- Proposed mechanism: signaling efficacy hypothesis — partial agonism
  below threshold needed for hallucinosis
- Structural basis: alpha-ethyl group clashes with W336/F340 in the
  orthosteric binding sub-pocket of 5-HT2A (PDB: 6WHA)
- 4C-TFM shows 8-fold 5-HT2A/2B selectivity — most cardiac-safe analog
- Ariadne rescues motor deficits in auxilin-KO Parkinson's mouse model
